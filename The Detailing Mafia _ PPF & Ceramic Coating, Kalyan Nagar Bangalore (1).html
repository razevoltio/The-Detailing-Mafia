Common questions

How long does a coating take?

Most cars need one to three days, depending on paint condition and the treatment chosen.

Do I need paint correction first?

Usually yes. A coating locks in whatever is on the paint, so we fix swirls before sealing.

Can I get a quote without visiting?

Yes. Send photos and your car model on WhatsApp and we will quote you.
